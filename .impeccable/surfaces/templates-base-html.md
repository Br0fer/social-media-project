# Surface: whole app shell and every template (Operate)

Scope: base layout plus all Django templates under `templates/`. Mode: **Operate**. Visitors are reviewers who click through as a demo user: they sign in, read the feed, and open profiles, groups and channels. They like, repost and comment, join groups, and add friends.
Constraints: no Python edits; templates and static files only. Text has to fit Ukrainian strings too. Supports light and dark themes, following the OS.
Direction source: chosen by the user, generated by ui-ux-pro-max ("Vibrant & Block-based": rose #E11D48 and blue #2563EB). The user picked Unbounded for headings and Inter for body text.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every surface tells you which social space you are in (a person, a group or a channel) through a solid block of that space's color. This replaces the category default: a gray Bootstrap feed with one blue accent.

OWN-WORLD: Rose-tinted paper ground with white content blocks and 20px corners. Each space has its own color, used at full strength: rose #E11D48 for people (also the brand), blue #2563EB for groups, violet #7C3AED for channels. Headings are Unbounded 700-800 and body text is Inter. Buttons are chunky pills, shadows are soft with a real offset, and icons come from Bootstrap Icons. The dark theme uses a deep ink-plum ground with the same three space colors, lifted for contrast.

STORY: The visitor understands at a glance where each post comes from. They open a person, a group or a channel and arrive inside that space's colored header, with the context and their own role clear. They act (like, repost, comment, join, befriend, subscribe), and every control answers the press immediately.

FIRST VIEWPORT (feed): A sticky top bar holds the Unbounded wordmark on the left, then Feed, Groups, Channels and People, then Requests, a rose "New post" pill and an avatar link on the right. Desktop has three columns: a rail with the signed-in user's profile block, a 640px feed of post blocks, and a rail with create-group and create-channel actions. Each post starts with an origin strip in its space's color. On mobile, a bottom tab bar replaces the nav, and "New post" stays one tap away.

FORM: User-pinned direction (ui-ux-pro-max design-system output), translated to an Operate shell. Concept-seed was not run because the user pinned the direction. Seed key: none.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
